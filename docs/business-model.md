# Business Model: Structural Steel Erection Crew Scheduling & Logistics Coordination Service

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7214`
- ISCO-08: `7214`
- Occupation: Structural Metal Preparers and Erectors
- Social impact: worker-safety, structure-durability, local-jobs

## Scope

**This actor coordinates job-site scheduling and logistics only.** It
never performs structural-steel erection work itself, never finalizes
a structural-steel-erection-execution decision, never authorizes a
crane lift, and never overrides a site-safety officer's judgment.
Structural metal preparers and erectors assemble steel-frame
structures at height on active job sites and handle crane-lifted
loads — one of the highest fall-risk/crane-hazard trades — so every
proposal this actor's advisor can make is limited to coordination,
not execution.

## Customer

- structural-steel erection contractors
- steel-erection crews and site foremen

## Offer

- work-record logging (task, materials usage, progress)
- crew/task/crane-schedule scheduling proposals
- safety-concern surfacing (fall hazard, crane condition, load hazard)
- structural-steel-materials supply-order coordination

## Revenue

- monthly coordination-platform retainer
- per-site logistics fee

## Trust Controls

- no structural-steel-erection-execution decision is ever finalized by this actor
- no crane lift is ever authorized by this actor
- no site-safety officer's judgment is ever overridden by this actor
- every safety-concern flag always escalates to human sign-off
- supply orders above the registered cost threshold always escalate to human sign-off
- job-site and crew-member provenance is independently verified before any coordination action
- coordination and audit records are auditable, not editable
